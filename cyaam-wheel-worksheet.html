Ebonics-first Feelings Wheel: second-ring worksheet

Use this research worksheet to organize the second ring without inventing or prematurely approving vocabulary. The rows are intentionally blank. Write only expressions you want to consider, record their meaning and context, and explicitly approve or reject each one.

Draft vocabulary, not a released wheel. This tool does not suggest or approve expressions automatically. Entries stay on this page unless you explicitly copy, print, or download a draft file. You can reopen that file on this device later. Nothing is transmitted to KUMBAYA or stored on its servers. Don't enter private medical histories or personal identifying details.

Second-ring vocabulary review

For each candidate, record the core emotion it belongs under, the expression and its nuance, regional/community usage notes, and your decision. If your source or context isn't confirmed, mark the entry for review.

Approval sequence
Candidate → explain the nuanced meaning → verify local/community usage → compare with nearby second-ring candidates → explicitly approve before entering the final wheel. Do not treat an empty or “needs review” entry as finalized Cyaam vocabulary.

How our services work

We want you to know what you can expect before sharing information, agreeing to work, or paying a fee.

1. Browse or ask about a service

Use the service finder to understand the options. Published prices describe potential offerings, not a guarantee that every listed service is immediately available. Currently unapproved, regulated or developing services remain unavailable.

2. Book a free introduction or request a quote

The 15-minute KUMBAYA introduction is free and nonclinical. For paid Right Hand Th’am work, send a non-sensitive request identifying the service and general task. An inquiry does not create a client agreement, booking, or paid obligation.

3. Review the scope and terms in writing

Before paid work, KUMBAYA must confirm that the specific nonclinical service is authorized and available, then identify the deliverable, included time or units, total price, necessary permissions, delivery method, and any service-specific cancellation or refund terms. Do not assume a universal price applies to additional work.

4. Pay only after accepting the quote

When a paid service is approved, KUMBAYA sends an itemized Stripe invoice matching the accepted quote. Payment card information must be entered only in Stripe's supported payment interface, not emailed or sent through a general contact form. KUMBAYA confirms a paid appointment or assignment after verifying payment and availability. Paid self-booking in Do’step is not enabled.

5. Receive the agreed service or referral

The provider delivers only the agreed nonclinical service or resource information. Changes and additional charges require written authorization. Requests for correction, rescheduling, or cancellation follow the terms disclosed for that particular service and applicable law.

What about counseling through Cyaam?

Cyaam independently develops original educational products, learning tools and planned nonclinical community events. These offerings are not psychotherapy. Counseling inquiries go directly to Rainbow Connection Counseling Collective (RCCC), which independently handles clinical intake, appointment scheduling, clinical fees, informed consent and patient records. KUMBAYA does not book or collect counseling fees for RCCC.

What about products, workshops and Process Haus?

Cyaam products and events listed as in development are not available to purchase or register for. Process Haus and other credential-gated services remain unavailable until required registrations, insurance and operating arrangements are confirmed. A request for information does not reserve an event or authorize regulated work.

Questions, cancellations or billing concerns

Contact kumbaya.cloud@proton.me with the non-sensitive service name and your preferred contact method. Any actual cancellation or refund policy must be provided as part of your individual written quote before payment. If you are already a client, refer to your accepted service terms and invoice.

Privacy reminder: Please do not send medical records, SSNs, identification photos, financial-account credentials, therapy information or other sensitive documents using ordinary email or public booking fields. Read our privacy and service-boundary notice.

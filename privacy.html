Privacy, inquiries & service boundaries

Do not send medical records, therapy histories, diagnoses, Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, government ID, or other sensitive records through the public contact form, ordinary email, or general booking questions. If you are seeking psychotherapy, use Rainbow Connection Counseling Collective’s own secure intake pathway.

Who provides which services?

Right Hand Th’am / KUMBAYA: independent nonclinical resource navigation, referral assistance, and administrative support, subject to each service's published scope and availability. We do not provide psychotherapy, legal advice, or unauthorized professional representation.

Cyaam / KUMBAYA: original educational resources, books, workbooks, learning tools, planned wellness events, and independent nonclinical programming. Product and event listings identify what is available versus still in development. Buying an educational resource or attending a nonclinical event does not create a therapist–client relationship.

Counseling: psychotherapy inquiries go to the separate practice Rainbow Connection Counseling Collective (RCCC). RCCC, not KUMBAYA, handles its own intake, clinical scheduling, informed consent, fees, medical records, and clinical privacy notices. You may ask RCCC about Daria Porter, Registered Associate Marriage and Family Therapist #146290; RCCC confirms fit and availability.

Information used to respond to you

If you contact KUMBAYA, request a quote, or book a free introduction, the information you choose to submit may include your name, contact details, requested service area, availability, and message. We use it to respond, understand the general request, coordinate an authorized service, and maintain relevant operational records. Please provide only the minimum necessary details at the initial inquiry stage.

Third-party systems

Public introductory bookings use Calendly. Payment requests and invoices, when offered, use Stripe. KUMBAYA's website hosting is provided through Render, and Do’step account authentication and authorized portal data are supported by Supabase. These providers may process information necessary for their respective services, subject to their own terms and privacy information. A provider connection does not by itself establish HIPAA suitability for KUMBAYA's portal.

Payments, pricing and appointments

The KUMBAYA 15-minute introduction is free. Paid nonclinical services are quoted or invoiced only after scope, availability, pricing, and authorization are confirmed. A quote is not a confirmed appointment; avoid sending card details by email. The service menu describes current fees, service restrictions, and cancellation/refund considerations. RCCC controls its own counseling fees.

Access, correction, and questions

For questions about KUMBAYA information, or to request correction of information you've provided, write to kumbaya.cloud@proton.me. Do not include sensitive identifiers or medical information in your first email. For counseling records or RCCC intake requests, contact RCCC directly.

This notice describes the website's intended information flows; it is not a certification of medical-data compliance. Before expanding Do’step to handle sensitive clinical records, obtain appropriate professional security and legal review.

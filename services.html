Binya Access Services

Services + pricing, without the maze.

Browse the current Los Angeles menu, search by service, or show only the options that can cost $0. Standard pricing and Community Access pricing are shown side by side so people can see the real cost before they ask for help.

Community Access

Open to anyone who self-attests that standard pricing would create a financial or practical access barrier. No protected identity is required, and self-attestation is normally enough.

Start with $0

The 15-minute “Where Do I Start?” triage and targeted referral match are free. Many safety, crisis, legal-referral, veteran, housing, harm-reduction, survivor, and community-resource options are also free.

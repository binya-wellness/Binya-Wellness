Sign-in and account help

Use this guide to identify the next step without sharing your password or verification codes. This page does not read, change, or test your account.

1. Signing in

  1. Open Do’step and enter the email associated with your existing KUMBAYA account.
  2. Enter your password. Public self-registration is not currently available.
  3. If prompted, enter the current code from your own authenticator app. Sensitive client information and team features require completed multi-factor authentication (MFA).

If the page says the secure backend is unavailable, don't keep submitting your password. Use the free introduction link below or contact KUMBAYA.

2. Forgot your password?

On Do’step, enter your existing account email and choose Reset password securely. Use the verification message sent to that email account. You can also open the account recovery page for the email-code option when configured.

Recovery messages and codes expire; use the newest one. If your recovery email doesn't arrive, check spam and make sure you're using the correct existing account email. Don't share screenshots of recovery links or codes.

3. Your authenticator isn't working

Confirm you're using the authenticator entry associated with your current Do’step account and that your phone's time is automatic. If you've lost the authenticator or changed devices without transferring it, contact KUMBAYA for account recovery assistance. Don't email the actual code or a QR setup secret.

For your security, this page does not bypass MFA or create a replacement administrator account.

4. What can I book or upload?

KUMBAYA's free 15-minute introduction is scheduled through Calendly. Paid services are not self-bookable. Nonclinical quote inquiries can be prepared without a Do’step login.

Sensitive document uploads are currently disabled. Please don't send clinical histories, government IDs, financial passwords, or other sensitive records through general email or public forms. Counseling inquiries belong with Rainbow Connection Counseling Collective.

Need help? Email kumbaya.cloud@proton.me with the general access issue and the email you want KUMBAYA to reply to. Never include passwords, one-time codes, recovery links, or medical records.
